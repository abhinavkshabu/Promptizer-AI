# Promptizer AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 9, 2026

## What Promptizer Does

Promptizer AI is a Chrome extension that optimizes prompts for AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude). When you click the Promptizer button, your prompt text is processed to create an improved version.

## Data We Collect

### Prompt Text (Processed, Not Stored)
When you click "Promptize," the text in your chat input field is sent to our server (`promptizer-ai.onrender.com`) for processing. **We do not store, log, or retain your prompts.** The text is sent to the Groq API for optimization and the result is returned directly to you.

### No Personal Information
We do not collect, store, or transmit:
- Your name, email, or account information
- Browsing history or browsing activity
- Cookies or tracking identifiers
- IP addresses for tracking purposes

### Local Storage
The extension stores your preferences (selected mode and persona) locally in Chrome's storage. This data never leaves your browser.

## Third-Party Services

### Groq API
When using the cloud backend, your prompt text is sent to [Groq](https://groq.com) for processing via their LLM API. Groq's privacy policy applies to this processing: [https://groq.com/privacy-policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy)

### Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano)
When available, Promptizer uses Chrome's on-device Gemini Nano model. In this case, **your prompt never leaves your device** — all processing happens locally.

## Data Security

- All network communication uses HTTPS encryption
- No API keys or credentials are stored in the extension
- We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the extension's support page on the Chrome Web Store.
